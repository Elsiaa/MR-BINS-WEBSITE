Goodquestions.

Open Sun–Thu 9–7 · Fri 9–5 · Sat closed for restock

What exactly is a bin store?

Truckloads of returns and overstock from Amazon and big-box retailers get delivered to us on pallets. We dump them, totally unsorted, into big blue bins, and everything in the store is one price that day. You dig through and find things worth far more than you pay.

How does the price work?

Simple: $14 on Sunday, and it drops a dollar every day down to $9 on Friday. Every item in the bins is the same price. Come early in the week for the best selection, or Friday for the lowest price.

Is everything brand new?

Most items are brand-new overstock or unopened returns. It’s a treasure hunt, so check anything you pick up before you buy. That’s part of the dig.

Why are you closed on Saturdays?

Saturday is restock day. The trucks come in, we dump fresh pallets into the bins, and Sunday morning the store opens stuffed full at $14. That’s why Sunday has the most to pick from.

Do I need a ticket or membership?

Nope. Just walk in. Sundays are the busiest, so come early if you want first crack at the fresh bins.
